Career

Ololade grew up in Lagos State and obtained her first degree in Theatre and Performing Arts from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Kaduna State. After that, she also studied Cinematography at the New York Film Academy. Ololade developed an interest in cinematography after her participation in a practical field workshop at the National Film Institute in Jos, Plateau State. She began her filmmaking career in 2005 and made her breakthrough with her award winning short film The Diary of Bolanle. Winning the Best Student Film at the Los Angeles Cinema Festival of Nollywood in California.

Expertise

Ololade is highly skilled in various aspects of filmmaking, including directing, producing, and cinematography. Her expertise lies in storytelling, visual composition, and bringing out authentic performances from actors. She has a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the technical aspects of film production, making her a versatile and accomplished filmmaker.
